# Windows panel alignment

Codex recorded these guides on 2026-11-26.

- The panel header and the cards use the panel inset.
- In Recent, the heading, date, and transcript use the same text line.
  The history row has 10 px of padding and a 1 px border.
- Clear history and the row copy buttons share a center line. The confirmation
  reuses both history insets for its text and buttons.
